Hi there!

I’m Kade Dean, a recent graduate from the University of Arizona with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. My journey into the world of technology began at a young age when I discovered my passion for the field. At the age of 9, I built my first computer (with quite a bit of help from my ex-NASA network engineer grandpa). This experience ignited a spark within me and set me on the path towards a deeper understanding of the computational systems that heavily occupy our daily lives.

Throughout my time at university, I had the opportunity to work on various projects that allowed me to apply my knowledge and skills in real-world scenarios. These experiences strengthened my foundation in computer science and fueled my passion for creating efficient and innovative solutions. Since graduating, I started KHD Software LLC and have been a self-employed full stack consultant. My recent contracts involve developing a bespoke AI-powered government policy tracker platform for a BioTech client along with other various full stack contracts. 

When I’m not busy with work, you’ll find me tinkering with my homelab setup and/or deep diving into whatever topic I'm interested in that week. I love diving deep into Linux systems, experimenting with different configurations, and learning about the inner workings of operating systems. I also love tinkering with hardware and have been super interested in embedded software development of late. This hands-on approach to technology not only satisfies my curiosity but also helps me develop a deeper understanding of the systems I work with daily.

My education at the University of Arizona provided me with a comprehensive understanding of computer science principles and their practical applications. In essence, my journey began as a childhood fascination and has blossomed into a driving force that shaped my education and now fuels my professional ambitions. I’m excited about the possibilities that lie ahead and eager to make meaningful contributions to the ever-evolving field of technology.
